# Style & Color Continuity Rule

When developing or modifying the UpScala website, you MUST adhere strictly to the established styles and color palette defined in `tailwind.config.js`.

1.  **Do not introduce new colors:** Stick to the existing `primary` (vibrant blue), `secondary` (light slate), `muted`, `navy`, `foreground`, and `background` colors.
2.  **Do not deviate from established component styles:** Use the existing reusable components (`Button`, `Navbar`, `Footer`, `ServiceCard`, `ProductCard`, `IndustryCard`) and their predefined variants instead of creating ad-hoc utility classes.
3.  **Typography:** Stick to the `Inter` font family and established font sizes/weights.
4.  **Icons:** Exclusively use `lucide-react` for any iconography.

This ensures a premium, cohesive, and continuous design aesthetic across the entire website.
